SolarWinds is expanding its observability capabilities for Nutanix environments with upcoming support for Nutanix Kubernetes Platform (NKP). As more organizations modernize with containers and cloud‑native applications, this integration will give IT teams a unified way to monitor Kubernetes workloads alongside their Nutanix infrastructure—all from a single SolarWinds platform. This addition builds on our long-standing native API integration with Nutanix, helping teams simplify operations as they adopt both virtualized and containerized architectures.
