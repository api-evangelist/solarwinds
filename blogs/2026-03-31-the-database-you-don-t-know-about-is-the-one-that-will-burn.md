It’s 2 a.m. The alert’s screaming. You’re SSH’d into the server, chasing a database performance incident, when you realize the offending database instance isn’t in your database monitoring tool. It never was. Someone spun up that database eighteen months ago for a “temporary” project, and it’s been sitting on your network ever since, unpatched, unmonitored, and now, apparently, on fire. This isn’t a hypothetical. For teams managing large numbers of database instances across sprawling, distributed infrastructure, rogue and unmonitored databases are an everyday reality.
