In today’s data-driven environments, the role of the database administrator (DBA) has never been more critical or more demanding. Modern database administration now spans on-premises data centers, Microsoft SQL Server, Oracle, and AWS-hosted databases, as well as third-party managed services. As organizations scale across cloud and hybrid architectures, DBAs are expected to maintain database performance, control costs, and ensure availability, often at the same time. It’s no surprise that DBA burnout is a growing concern.
