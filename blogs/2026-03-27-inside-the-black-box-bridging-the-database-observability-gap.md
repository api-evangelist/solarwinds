Over the past 15 years, Agile and DevOps have transformed how organizations deliver software. Agile emphasizes iterative development, collaboration, and continuous feedback, while DevOps brings development and operations together to automate and accelerate software delivery. Teams now rely on rapid iterations, shared responsibility, and automation. Yet in many organizations, databases remain isolated from these practices, often treated as a separate layer rather than fully integrated into the software delivery process.
